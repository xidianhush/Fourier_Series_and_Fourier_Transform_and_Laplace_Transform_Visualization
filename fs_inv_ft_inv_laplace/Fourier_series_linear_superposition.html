Why do all three views end at the same tip?

The complex-exponential coefficients of a real signal are always conjugate-symmetric: c−k = ck*. Hence
Σk=−N…N ckejkω₀t = c0 + Σk=1…N 2Re{ckejkω₀t} = A0 + Σ Akcos(kω₀t+φk), which is always real.
The "one-sided rotating chain" doubles the weights (wk = 2ck, k≥1) and keeps only k≥0, giving a genuinely rotating, scaling complex vector F(t), while x(t) = Re{F(t)} is exactly the same as above — and that is the curve drawn in the lower panel.
