Signal & period (hero slider: T)

Extend the aperiodic x(t) periodically with period T into xT(t). Small T: the copies overlap — a periodic signal with sparse, tall spectral lines. Large T: the copies leave the window, Δω=2π/T grows dense, Fn·T hugs the envelope |X(jω)|, and the Riemann sum becomes the inverse-transform integral.
